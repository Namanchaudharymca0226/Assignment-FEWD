My Favorite Place: Landour

Landour is a charming hill station in Uttarakhand, India. Its quiet lanes, old colonial-era buildings, and mountain views make it special. This peaceful hillside town sits above Mussoorie, in the Himalayan foothills.

About the Place

Landour is a small cantonment town near Mussoorie, known for its forested slopes and views of the Himalayas.
Take a slow walk along its winding roads and enjoy the calm mountain atmosphere.

Location: Landour, near Mussoorie, Uttarakhand, India
Best time to visit: March to June or September to November
Famous for: Himalayan views, quiet walks, and colonial-era charm

Attractions and Features

  • Lal Tibba viewpoint and mountain panoramas
  • Char Dukan's small cafes and local treats
  • Landour Bazaar and historic churches

Things to Do

  1. Walk the quiet roads and forest trails
  2. Enjoy tea or a snack at Char Dukan
  3. Take in the Himalayan scenery and photograph the views
